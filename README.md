# Overview
Retro-Bridge connects your devices to your RetroTINK wirelessly. It serves as a Wi-Fi bridge for serial communication with RetroTINK 4K and 6X scalers, without the need for physical cables.

Based on a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W, Retro-Bridge connects to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, and to your RetroTINK via USB or HD-15.
